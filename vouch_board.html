Vouch Technologies, Inc.

Executive Board & Organizational Network Charter

Mission: Democratizing enterprise-grade inbox security and crowdsourced threat intelligence for every human inbox on Earth — without fear, jargon, or friction.

1. Executive Leadership (C-Suite)

👑 Chief Executive Officer (CEO) — The Founder

  • Primary Mandate: Company vision, capital allocation, investor relations, culture, and ultimate authority on product direction and ethical standards.
  • Core Philosophy: "Security tools shouldn't make users feel stupid or panicked. We build for our families first."
  • Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Weekly Active Protected Inboxes (WAPI), 30-day tester retention rate, capital efficiency and burn runway.

🛡️ Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

  • Primary Mandate: Total data sovereignty, OAuth scope governance, zero-knowledge architecture, and adversarial threat defense.
  • Core Philosophy: "We only ask for read-only access, we never sell raw email bodies, and we encrypt everything at rest."
  • Key Responsibilities: Supervise Blue Team and Red Team operations, oversee CASA Tier 2 & Google OAuth Security Review compliance, audit cryptographic standards (AES-256-GCM, HMAC-SHA256).
  • KPIs: Zero unauthorized token exposures, MTTD and MTTR < 2 hours for critical vulnerabilities, 100% compliance with Google Restricted Scope API policies.

💻 Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

  • Primary Mandate: Systems scalability, database architecture, latency optimization, and core threat engine accuracy.
  • Core Philosophy: "Zero-compile on production, resilient dual-engine fallbacks, sub-500ms response times."
  • Key Responsibilities: Full-stack roadmap execution (Node.js, Neon PostgreSQL, React), database connection pooling, LLM cost optimization.
  • KPIs: 99.95% API uptime, P95 scan latency < 1,500ms for 30 emails, zero connection pool exhaustion.

📦 Chief Product Officer (CPO)

  • Primary Mandate: User journey friction removal, feature discovery, testing cadence, and behavioral engagement.
  • Core Philosophy: "If a user has to read a manual or clicks 'cancel' on the Google warning screen, the product failed, not the user."
  • KPIs: Connect Gmail conversion rate > 65%, Action completion rate > 40%, Net Promoter Score (NPS) > 60.

🚀 Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

  • Primary Mandate: Brand positioning, community flywheel, press/influencer relations, product-led growth (PLG).
  • Core Philosophy: "Vouch is not an antivirus utility; it is the collective radar that keeps the neighborhood safe."
  • KPIs: Organic invite viral coefficient (K-factor) > 1.2, Cost Per Protected Inbox < $0.50 during public beta.

2. Cybersecurity Division

🔵 Blue Team Lead (Defensive Architecture & Incident Response)

  • Mission: Fortify all perimeter and data layers; ensure zero unauthenticated token leaks or privilege escalations.
  • Active Defenses:
    • Rate Limiting: Tiered token bucket limits on /api/auth, /api/emails, and global endpoints.
    • Cryptographic Enclaves: AES-256-GCM authenticated cipher with distinct IVs for refresh and access tokens.
    • Session Integrity: Cryptographic HMAC signature verification on auth state, eliminating parameter tampering.

🔴 Red Team Lead (Adversarial Emulation & Penetration)

  • Mission: Continuously attack Vouch's threat engine, APIs, and consensus logic to expose weaknesses before real adversaries do.
  • Ongoing Drills: Prompt Injection Testing, Consensus Poisoning / Sybil Network Simulations, BOLA / IDOR automated endpoint fuzzing.

⚖️ Trust & Safety Lead (Anti-Poisoning & Consensus Defense)

  • Mission: Maintain the mathematical integrity of crowdsourced voting.
  • Mechanisms: User reputation weighting, distinct-identity quorum thresholds, whitelist shields for authenticated big domains (google.com, apple.com).

3. Engineering & Systems Division

  • 🐘 Principal Backend & Database Architect: Node.js Express, Neon Serverless PostgreSQL pool, sub-10ms index queries on scans and community_fingerprints, dual-engine PostgreSQL/SQLite fallback.
  • ⚡ Principal Frontend Architect: React 18, Tailwind CSS, pre-compiled static assets with content hashing ([name]-[hash].js), robust AbortController state management.
  • ☁️ Platform Reliability & DevOps Lead (SRE): Render cloud infrastructure, Cloudflare edge routing, zero-downtime deploys, automated health probes.

4. Product, Design & Editorial Division

  • 📋 Production & Release Operations Manager: Manages the 100-tester Google OAuth closed alpha cohort, tester briefings, and Stage 1.0 release gates.
  • 🎨 Head of Product Design & Brand Identity: Warm editorial palette (Teal #2E5C55, Sage #7FA085, Paper #F7F8F7), elegant Fraunces serif headings, distraction-free CTAs.
  • ✨ Lead UI/UX Designer & Systems Architect: Anti-anxiety visual language (calm pulsing indicators instead of panic sirens), thumb-friendly mobile ergonomics (≥44px touch targets), fluid CSS grid accordions, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
  • ✍️ Editorial & Behavioral Psychology Lead: Non-intimidating, conversational explanations under 60 words that explain threats without cybersecurity jargon.

5. External Legal Counsel & Corporate Compliance

  • Google Limited Use & Privacy Policy Compliance: Enforces zero sale of raw email data, zero training of public LLMs on emails, ephemeral metadata processing under Google Restricted Scope guidelines.
  • Product Liability & Zero-Warranty Disclaimers: Comprehensive cybersecurity indemnification clauses protecting founders from liability for edge-case social engineering attacks.
  • IP & Trademark Protection: USPTO Trademark filings for the Vouch brand/shield logo (Class 042) and provisional patent protection for crowdsourced domain hashing.
  • Corporate Governance: Incorporation of Vouch Technologies, Inc. as a Delaware C-Corporation with standard 4-year founder equity vesting.

7. Weekly Operating Cadence

  • Monday Morning Sprint Kickoff (30 min): Inboxes protected review, false positive triage, threat heuristic prioritization.
  • Wednesday Red vs. Blue Adversarial Review (45 min): Red team presents new spoofing vectors; Blue team validates verified mitigations.
  • Friday Release Review (20 min): Deploy sanity check, Render logs, Neon pool telemetry, and next tester batch approval.
  • Monthly Executive Governance Board: Burn rate, Google verification milestone, custom domain acquisition, and Public Beta launch metrics.
